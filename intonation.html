🎵 Pitch Accent & Intonation

Japanese does not use STRESS like English — it uses PITCH (high and low).
Master the rules below, then test your ear with the practice quiz!

🌼 Score: 0 / 16

🔑 The 3 Absolute Rules

1
Mora 1 and mora 2 always have DIFFERENT pitch.
If the 1st mora is low, the 2nd must be high — and vice versa. (e.g. あなた vs あなた)
2
Once the pitch falls, it NEVER rises again within the same word.
A word can only have ONE pitch "mountain". After the drop, it stays low until the word ends.
3
Think of particles（が・は・を…）as part of the set.
Two words can sound identical alone, but the pitch of the following particle reveals the difference (橋が vs 箸が).

📊 The 4 Basic Patterns

PatternShapeExamplePitch (L = low, H = high)
平板型
Heiban		1st mora low, rises from the 2nd, particle stays HIGH.水 (mizu)L - H (particle H)
頭高型
Atamadaka		Only the 1st mora is high; everything after is low.箸 (hashi)H - L (particle L)
中高型
Nakadaka		Rises in the middle, then falls. Particle low.あなた (anata)L - H - L (particle L)
尾高型
Odaka		High until the end of the word, but the PARTICLE drops.橋 (hashi)L - H (particle L)
